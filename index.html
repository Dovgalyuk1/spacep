    MISSION STATEMENT

    MAKE PONS
    MULTIPLANETARY

    a new space company with one mission. we are sending the first pons to mars, building p city, launching parlink and putting peslas on martian roads. earth was only the beginning.

    i founded the company and then i hired myself as the chief engineer, the ground crew, the safety officer and the guy who says go. every meeting is me standing on the pad talking out loud, and every proposal passes unanimously, because everyone in the room agrees with me for reasons i consider obvious.

    the rocket fires the entire time, under my arm, and i have never once climbed into it. earth was only the beginning, but somebody has to stay down here holding the thing while the beginning finishes, and that somebody has a cigar, a schedule and no intention of sharing either.

    first a pons lands on mars, then p city gets poured around wherever it lands, then parlink covers the sky and peslas fill roads that do not exist yet. the order is not negotiable, the dates are extremely negotiable, and the announcement always ships before the hardware does.

    eventually i bought the alphabet outright, because owning the letters is cheaper than asking permission to use them. that is how a rocket, a city, a satellite network and a car company all ended up starting with the same letter, and nobody has yet explained to me why that is supposed to be a problem.
